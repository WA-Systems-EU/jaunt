The choices

Why these pieces

Why Hetzner?

Hetzner is a European company with data centres in Germany and Finland, and its prices make a box sized for the day affordable: servers are billed by the hour, so a box that runs only while you work costs little (see the cost estimates). Its API covers everything jauntbox needs (servers, volumes, firewalls and SSH keys) and has a good Go library.

Hetzner is the only provider for now. The code talks to it through a provider interface, so other providers can be added later; Scaleway is the first candidate.

Why Tailscale?

So the box has no public ports at all. Its cloud firewall blocks every incoming connection, and you reach it only over your tailnet, by a stable name: jauntbox-<you>.

Tailscale SSH ties logins to your tailnet identity, and your tailnet policy decides who may reach the box as root. Tailscale's free personal plan is enough. Headscale (self-hosted Tailscale) and NetBird (German, open source) are on the roadmap.

How is this different from Codespaces, Gitpod or Coder?

The box is your own server, in your own Hetzner project, on your own tailnet: no vendor sits between you and your code. Each coding agent runs locked in its own container on the box, with a token for one repository at a time. And the box deletes itself when you stop working, so it costs almost nothing while you are away (only the small volume).

Why only GitHub?

GitHub is the first forge adapter: a private GitHub App mints short-lived tokens for one repository at a time. Gitea and Forgejo adapters are on the roadmap.

Using it

Day to day

What happens to my work when the box deletes itself?

Your /home lives on a separate volume that jauntbox never deletes. The next jauntbox up attaches it again, with your checkouts, shell history and settings.

What does it cost?

Only the hours the server runs, plus the small volume. See the cost estimates on the home page. The box cannot keep billing overnight unless you ask it to with jauntbox night.

What if I forget to turn it off?

It deletes itself at the evening cutoff, after a maximum uptime, or once you have been idle, unless you are actively working or allowed a night session. A safety net deletes a box whose supervisor stops reporting. See limits and self-delete.

Which coding agents work?

Claude Code for now, with a Claude subscription token (claude setup-token). Anthropic API keys are not supported yet.

What do I need on my laptop?

The jauntbox binary (built from source during the beta), Tailscale and OpenSSH. We use it from Linux and GhostBSD; macOS and Windows are untested. The box itself runs Linux on amd64.

Can my team share a box?

No: jauntbox is one box per person. Team features (a hosted control plane, onboarding and shared policies) are the enterprise edition, coming soon.
